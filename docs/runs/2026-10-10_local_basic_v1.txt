1. The decision
---------------
  297 Cleveland chest-pain patients (137 with heart disease), 13 possible measurements.
  The clinic can collect only 5. Tests have real prices (Turney 1995, Ontario fee schedule 1992, CAD),
  and some share one procedure: pay for the procedure once, the extra readings are almost free.
  shared procedure     | tests                 | paid once
  ---------------------+-----------------------+----------
  blood draw           | chol, fbs             | $2.10    
  thallium stress test | thalach, thal         | $101.90  
  treadmill ECG        | exang, oldpeak, slope | $86.30   
  price self-test: polynomial vs table on all 8,192 subsets, max difference 1.4e-14

6. What this does not claim
---------------------------
  * No advantage over exhaustive search: at 13 tests it is exact and takes milliseconds (section 2).
  * No speedup. The quantum job is compared on sample quality at equal numbers of samples.
  * Prices are 1992 Ontario fees, used as relative costs; patients are a referral cohort, not screening.
  * The AUC front is the best of 1,287 panels on all patients (optimistic); re-selecting the panel
    inside each cross-validation fold gives lower, honest numbers (project page, 'Results').
